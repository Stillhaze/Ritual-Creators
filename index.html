About the Community

Ritual is a lab for autonomous intelligence — building the infrastructure that lets autonomous agents persist, hold and spend their own money, keep secrets under adversarial conditions, and keep operating long after the person who launched them has moved on. That work spans four research frontiers — Artificial Intelligence, Mechanism Design, Systems, and Cryptography — and comes together in Ritual Chain, the execution layer where that autonomy becomes real.

This page belongs to the people building that future alongside the core team: the Ritual Creators. Ritualists, Rittys, Radiant Ritualists, Mods, and Team members who share ideas, make content, and grow the community every day across Discord, X, and beyond. Every creator on this page is a real member of that community.
